# Privacy Policy – Session Board

Author: Marco Hallmeier

Session Board runs entirely on your own computer inside the desktop app.

- **No data leaves your computer.** The plugin sends nothing to the author, to 4vex or to any other service.
- **No data is stored.** The plugin keeps the session list only in memory while its panel is open.
- **What it reads:** the session list the desktop app provides to plugins, and the app's local session files on
  your computer to show each session's status.
- **What it runs:** your operating system's standard command to open a session link (`rundll32` on Windows,
  `open` on macOS, `xdg-open` on Linux), and on Windows `reg query` to read your display language.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/4vex-de/session-board/issues

## Datenschutz (Deutsch)

Session Board läuft vollständig auf Ihrem Rechner in der Desktop-App. Es sendet keine Daten an den Autor, an 4vex
oder an andere Dienste und speichert nichts dauerhaft. Gelesen werden nur die Sitzungsliste der App und die lokalen
Sitzungsdateien der App, um den Status anzuzeigen.
